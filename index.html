Biography

Yinan Xu is currently an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He received his Ph.D. in Computer System Architecture from ICT, CAS in 2025, advised by Prof. Ninghui Sun and Prof. Yungang Bao. He obtained his B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in 2019, advised by Prof. Yungang Bao. He was an exchange student at National University of Singapore (NUS) in 2018.

His research interests include computer architecture, agile chip development and verification, processor microarchitecture, open-source chip ecosystems, and hardware-software co-design. He is a core developer of the XiangShan open-source high-performance RISC-V processor, where he has pioneered several agile design and verification techniques that have been successfully integrated into the project. His work has been published in top venues such as MICRO, ISCA, HPCA, and DAC. XiangShan and its agile design methodology were selected as one of IEEE Micro Top Picks From the 2022 Computer Architecture Conferences, recognizing it as one of the year's most influential conference papers to computer architecture.

I am looking for self-motivated Ph.D. and master's students, as well as interns, interested in agile hardware development (including design, verification, performance evaluation/analysis, etc.), processor microarchitecture, hardware-software co-design, and datacenter architecture. Feel free to contact me by email if you are interested in doing research with us.

I am also leading the design and RTL development team of XiangShan at the Beijing Institute of Open Source Chip (BOSC). If you are interested in building high-performance RISC-V processors, feel free to contact me for both full-time and internship opportunities.
